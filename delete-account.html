Delete your Budgetly account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Budgetly and tap your initial at the top of the Home screen to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email us from the address you signed up with. Include your Budgetly username, if you know it.

Email sv7n.sa@gmail.com

We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: email address, username and display name.
  • All entries, categories and budgets.
  • Friends, split bills and IOUs.
  • Friendships, circle memberships, challenge progress and sharing settings.
  • Circles you own, and shared bills you created (for everyone on them).
  • Your share on other people’s shared bills, and payments recorded between you and others.

What is kept

  • Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall Budgetly or use Settings → Erase everything.

We don’t keep backups of deleted accounts beyond 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
