Budgetly

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the Budgetly app ("Budgetly", "we") collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have. Budgetly is a personal budget, expense and income tracker for Android and iPhone.

In short
  • You can use Budgetly without an account. Your data then stays on your phone.
  • If you create an account, your money data is stored in your account so it can be backed up and used on more than one phone.
  • Bank text messages are read on your phone and are never uploaded. Only the entries they create are.
  • Your location is never uploaded.
  • We don't show ads, don't sell your data, and don't use analytics or tracking tools.

1. Using Budgetly without an account

Everything you enter, including income, expenses, budgets, categories, friends, split bills and IOUs, is stored only on your phone. We receive none of it. Uninstalling the app or using “Erase everything” in Settings deletes it.

2. Data stored in your account

Signing in is optional. When you create an account, we store:

DataWhy
Email address, and your name from Google or Apple if you sign in with themTo create your account, sign you in and send account emails such as confirmation and password reset.
Username, display name, currencySo friends can find you, and so the app shows amounts correctly.
Income and expense entries (amount, category, short note, date), categories and budgetsTo back them up and keep them the same on all your phones.
Friends you add by name, with an optional phone number, plus split bills and IOUs (who owes what)To back them up and keep them the same on all your phones. The phone number is used only to open WhatsApp when you choose to send someone their share.
Budgetly friends, circles, challenges, group goals, shared bills and payments between friendsTo provide the social features you choose to use.

Other people see your data only where you choose to share it:

  • Friends: see your username and display name.
  • Shared bills: people on a shared bill see that bill.
  • Circles: members see only what you switch on under Compare, such as the percentage of your budget used. They never see your balances or individual entries.

3. Bank text messages (Android)

If you turn on “Read bank messages”, Budgetly asks for permission to read SMS. It then reads text messages on your phone to find bank transactions (purchases, transfers, refunds, salary) and adds them as entries.

  • Messages are processed only on your phone. The text of your messages is never uploaded, shared or stored outside the phone.
  • One-time codes, offers, declined payments and messages from personal phone numbers are ignored.
  • If you are signed in, the resulting entries (amount, shop or sender name, date, category) are backed up like any other entry.
  • You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Bank SMS, and remove the permission in Android settings.

On iPhone, Budgetly cannot read messages. If you set up a Shortcuts automation, it passes the text of a bank message, or an Apple Pay amount and shop name, to Budgetly on your phone, and Budgetly handles it the same way.

4. Location

If you turn on “Remember where I spend”, Budgetly records your location when you add an expense while the app is open, so it can show the Spending Map. It never uses location in the background.

Locations are stored only on your phone and are not uploaded to your account. To draw the map, your phone downloads map images from OpenFreeMap (based on OpenStreetMap). Like any website, OpenFreeMap can see your IP address and the map area being viewed.

5. Other data on your phone

  • App lock: your passcode is stored only as a salted hash, in your phone’s secure storage.
  • Fingerprint and Face ID: handled entirely by your phone. Budgetly never receives fingerprint or face data.
  • Phone-only settings: notification settings, repeating entries and SMS settings stay on the phone.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts accounts and synced data on servers in the European Union (Ireland), encrypted in transit and at rest. Every table is protected so each user can only read their own data and what others chose to share with them.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They share your name and email address with us. Apple can hide your real email address.
  • OpenFreeMap provides the map images (see section 4).
  • WhatsApp opens only when you tap “Send on WhatsApp”; you see the message before sending.

We don’t sell or rent your data, don’t use it for advertising, and don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

7. How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and everything stored with it within 30 days. That includes entries, friends, IOUs, circles you own, challenge progress, and shared bills you created, which are removed for everyone on them. Your share on other people’s bills is removed too.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See, edit, export (Settings → Export to CSV) and delete your entries in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Or ask us by email; see Delete your account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We reply within 30 days.

9. Children

Budgetly is meant for adults and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules on every database table, and the optional app lock. No system is perfectly secure. If you think your account has been accessed without your permission, contact us.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
